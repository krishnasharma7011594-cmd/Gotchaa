# GOTCHAA Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 3.0 — May 2026**  
**Effective Date:** May 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 21, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs technical and commercial use of the GOTCHAA Platform. Violations may result in suspension or termination.

## 1. Scope

Applies to all users, developers, and automated systems interacting with GOTCHAA APIs, clients, or infrastructure.

## 2. Prohibited Automated Access

You may **not**, without prior written permission:

- Scrape profiles, messages, or feeds  
- Use bots, crawlers, or scripts to create accounts or send messages  
- Reverse engineer client apps to bypass rate limits  
- Load test or DDoS our infrastructure  
- Harvest user data for commercial databases  

## 3. API and Cloud Function Abuse

- Respect Firebase and Cloud Function rate limits (e.g., VibeTalk queue: 10 joins/hour)  
- Do not probe, fuzz, or exploit undocumented endpoints  
- Do not share App Check bypass methods  
- Report vulnerabilities to security@gotchaa.com (see Vulnerability Disclosure Policy)  

## 4. Virtual Economy Abuse

- No real-money trading of Karma  
- No multi-account farming of Karma or invites  
- No fraudulent chargebacks on paid features  

## 5. Impersonation and Misrepresentation

- Do not impersonate GOTCHAA staff, celebrities, or other users  
- Do not use misleading usernames designed to defraud  
- Verified badges are system-assigned only  

## 6. Security

- No uploading malware or exploit code  
- No attempting to access `users_private` or other users' data  
- No sharing account credentials  

## 7. Enforcement

Violations → warning, restriction, permanent ban, and legal action where appropriate.

## 8. Contact

legal@gotchaa.com | security@gotchaa.com
