# GOTCHAA Community Guidelines

**Version 3.0 — May 2026**  
**Effective Date:** May 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 21, 2026

These Community Guidelines explain what we expect from every GOTCHAA user. They supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 1. Our Standards

GOTCHAA is built for connection across languages and cultures. Everyone deserves safety and respect.

## 2. Zero Tolerance

The following result in **immediate account termination** and reporting to authorities where required:

| Violation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) | Permanent ban + law enforcement report |
| Terrorism or violent extremism promotion | Permanent ban + report |
| Credible threats of violence | Permanent ban + report |
| Severe hate speech targeting protected groups | Permanent ban |

There is **no appeal** for confirmed CSAM or terrorism violations.

## 3. Prohibited Content

- Harassment, bullying, stalking, or doxxing  
- Non-consensual intimate imagery  
- Spam, scams, phishing, or impersonation  
- Sale of illegal goods or services  
- Malware or harmful links  
- Content violating others' intellectual property  

## 4. Enforcement Ladder

| Step | Typical action |
|------|----------------|
| 1 — First minor violation | Warning + content removal |
| 2 — Repeat or moderate violation | Feature restriction (24h–7d) |
| 3 — Serious violation | Account suspension (7–30 days) |
| 4 — Severe or repeat serious | Permanent ban |

We may skip steps for severe violations. Automated systems may flag content for human review.

## 5. Appeals

Email **appeals@gotchaa.com** within **14 days** with your username, action ID (if any), and explanation. We aim to respond within **14 business days**.

## 6. VibeTalk-Specific Rules

- Adults (18+) only — age tier verified server-side  
- No nudity, sexual content involving minors, or illegal activity  
- No recording or distribution of matches without consent where unlawful  
- Use Report during sessions for immediate safety review  
- Accept rate limits and cooldowns  

## 7. Virtual Economy (Karma)

- Do not buy, sell, or trade Karma for real money  
- Do not use bots or exploits to inflate Karma  
- Karma manipulation may result in Karma reset and ban  

## 8. Reporting

Use in-app **Report** on posts, profiles, chats, or VibeTalk sessions. For emergencies involving imminent harm, contact local authorities first, then trust@gotchaa.com.

## 9. Contact

**trust@gotchaa.com** | **appeals@gotchaa.com**
