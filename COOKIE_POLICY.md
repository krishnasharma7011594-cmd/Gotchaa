# GOTCHAA Cookie Policy

**Version 3.0 — May 2026**  
**Effective Date:** May 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 21, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how GOTCHAA uses cookies and similar technologies on the Platform.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (local storage, secure storage, device identifiers) store small amounts of data on your device to operate and improve services.

## 2. Cookies GOTCHAA Uses

| Category | Technology | Purpose | Can disable? |
|----------|------------|---------|--------------|
| Strictly necessary | Firebase Auth tokens, `flutter_secure_storage`, session prefs | Login, security, consent records | No — required |
| Functional | `shared_preferences` | Language, theme, settings | Partially via device |
| Analytics | Firebase Analytics | Usage statistics | **Yes — Settings → Privacy & Data** |
| Performance | Firebase Performance | App speed monitoring | **Yes — consent** |
| Crash | Firebase Crashlytics | Crash reporting | Legitimate interest (always on for stability) |

We request **explicit consent** before enabling Analytics and Performance (GDPR/ePrivacy).

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

When you open **external websites** (device browser) or **embedded service pages** for partners (e.g., delivery, transport):

- Those providers set **their own** cookies  
- GOTCHAA **does not** access or control those cookies  
- GOTCHAA **does not** collect browsing data from those sessions  
- Review each provider's cookie policy  

**WebView / external browser disclaimer:** Leaving GOTCHAA's core app experience means third-party cookie rules apply — not this policy.

## 4. How to Manage Cookies

- **In-app:** Settings → Privacy & Data (analytics, performance toggles)  
- **Device:** Reset advertising ID, clear app data, OS privacy settings  
- **Email:** privacy@gotchaa.com for access/deletion requests  

## 5. Do Not Track

We do not respond to DNT browser signals; we honor applicable opt-out rights under CCPA and GDPR via in-app controls.

Changes appear in the Legal Hub with version updates.
