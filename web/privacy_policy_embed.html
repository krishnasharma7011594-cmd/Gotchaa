Welcome to GOTCHAA. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how GOTCHAA ('we', 'us', or 'our') collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal data when you use our platform, including our mobile application and related services (collectively, the 'Platform').

This policy is designed to comply with major global privacy frameworks, including the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) / California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Australia's Privacy Act 1988, and other applicable data protection laws.

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

GOTCHAA operates as the data controller for personal data collected through the Platform. Our primary contact for privacy matters is:

  • Email: privacy@gotchaa.com
  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): dpo@gotchaa.com
  • Support: help.gotchaa.com

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our EU/UK representative can be reached at the same contact addresses. We will respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register or use GOTCHAA, we collect information you provide voluntarily, including:

  • Account information: email address, display name, username/handle, and password
  • Profile data: profile picture, bio, and language preferences
  • Content you create: posts, messages, comments, and other in-app communications
  • Communications with us: when you contact support or submit feedback

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Platform, we automatically collect certain technical data:

  • Device information: device type, operating system, browser type, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage data: features used, pages viewed, interactions, and session duration
  • Log data: IP addresses, access timestamps, crash reports, and error logs
  • Location data: approximate location derived from IP address (we do not collect precise GPS location without your explicit consent)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources such as:

  • Authentication providers (e.g., Google, Apple Sign-In) if you use social login
  • Analytics partners who help us understand platform usage patterns
  • Payment processors for transaction verification

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / LGPD / DPDPA)

We only process your personal data when we have a valid legal basis to do so. Depending on the purpose and your jurisdiction, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contractual necessity: Processing required to fulfill our agreement with you (e.g., providing the Platform, processing transactions)
  • Legitimate interests: Processing necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as improving the Platform, preventing fraud, and ensuring security — where these do not override your rights
  • Consent: Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
  • Legal obligation: Processing required to comply with applicable laws or court orders
  • Vital interests: In rare cases, to protect the safety of you or another person

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: To create and manage your account, enable platform features, and facilitate multilingual matching
  • Communication: To send you service-related notifications, account alerts, and (with your consent) promotional communications
  • Safety and security: To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, harassment, and violations of our Terms of Service
  • Platform improvement: To analyze usage trends, conduct research, and develop new features
  • Legal compliance: To meet our obligations under applicable laws and regulations
  • Dispute resolution: To enforce our agreements and resolve disputes

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects without your explicit consent.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

GOTCHAA does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. This applies to all users globally, including those covered by the CCPA/CPRA.

5.2 When We Share Information

We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted vendors who process data on our behalf to operate the Platform (e.g., Firebase for database infrastructure, cloud hosting providers, payment processors, analytics tools). All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements and are required to maintain appropriate security measures
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you prior to any such transfer and provide options where required by law
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by valid legal process (e.g., court order, subpoena) or to protect the rights, safety, or property of GOTCHAA, our users, or the public
  • With your consent: We may share your information with other parties when you have given us explicit consent to do so
  • Aggregated/anonymized data: We may share aggregated, de-identified data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you

6. International Data Transfers

GOTCHAA operates globally and may transfer your personal data to countries other than the one in which you reside. Where we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, for transfers from the EEA/UK
  • Adequacy decisions by relevant data protection authorities
  • Binding corporate rules or equivalent mechanisms as required by local law

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that your data may be processed in countries with different data protection standards. We take steps to ensure that any international transfers are conducted securely and in compliance with applicable law.

7. Data Security and Storage

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent)
  • Access controls: Role-based access, multi-factor authentication for administrative systems, and the principle of least privilege
  • Infrastructure: Enterprise-grade cloud architecture with regular security audits, vulnerability testing, and penetration testing
  • Incident response: Documented breach notification procedures — we will notify affected users and relevant authorities within 72 hours of becoming aware of a qualifying data breach (as required under GDPR and other applicable laws)

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal data using commercially acceptable means, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained for the duration of your account and deleted within 90 days of account deletion request
  • Transaction records: Retained for up to 7 years for tax and legal compliance purposes
  • Communications: Retained for a reasonable period to resolve disputes or as required by law
  • Logs and analytics: Typically retained in identifiable form for no longer than 24 months, after which data is aggregated or anonymized

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time (see Section 9 — Your Rights).

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

9.1 Rights for All Users

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal data ('right to be forgotten'), subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

9.2 Additional Rights (EEA/UK — GDPR/UK GDPR)

  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Rights related to automated decision-making: Not to be subject to solely automated decisions that significantly affect you

9.3 Additional Rights (California — CCPA/CPRA)

  • Right to know: Know what categories of personal information we collect and how it is used or shared
  • Right to opt-out of sale/sharing: Opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information (note: we do not sell your data)
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information: Limit our use of sensitive personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

9.4 Additional Rights (India — DPDPA 2023)

  • Right to information about processing of your personal data
  • Right to correction and erasure of personal data
  • Right to grievance redressal: Contact our Data Protection Officer for any privacy concerns
  • Right to nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the rights described above, please contact us at privacy@gotchaa.com or through the Help Center within the app. We will verify your identity before processing requests and will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally 30 days, with a possible extension for complex requests).

10. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

GOTCHAA uses cookies and similar technologies (e.g., local storage, device fingerprinting) to operate the Platform, remember your preferences, and analyze usage. You can manage your cookie preferences through your device settings or our in-app preferences center.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary: Essential for the Platform to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Performance/Analytics: Help us understand how users interact with the Platform (e.g., Firebase Analytics). These require your consent where applicable.
  • Functional: Enable enhanced features and personalization.

Where required by law (e.g., in the EEA under ePrivacy rules), we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

11. Children's Privacy COPPA Compliant

GOTCHAA is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, which may be up to 16 in certain countries under GDPR). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable minimum age.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us at privacy@gotchaa.com and we will promptly delete such data. Parents or guardians who become aware that their child has provided us with personal data without their consent should contact us immediately.
