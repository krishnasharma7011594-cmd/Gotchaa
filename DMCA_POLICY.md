GOTCHAA respects intellectual property rights and responds to valid notices under the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and equivalent laws.

## 1. Designated Agent

**DMCA Agent:** legal@gotchaa.com  
**Subject line:** "DMCA Takedown Notice"

## 2. Takedown Notice — Required Information

Per 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your notice must include:

1. Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent  
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed infringed  
3. Identification of the infringing material on GOTCHAA (URL, username, post ID, Vybz ID)  
4. Your contact information (address, phone, email)  
5. A statement of good-faith belief that use is not authorized  
6. A statement under penalty of perjury that information is accurate and you are authorized to act  
7. (If applicable) Statement that you are the rights holder or agent  

## 3. Our Process

1. Acknowledge receipt within **5 business days**  
2. Remove or disable access to allegedly infringing material  
3. Notify the uploader with reason for removal  
4. Maintain a record for repeat-infringer policy  

## 4. Counter-Notice

If you believe content was wrongly removed, send a counter-notice to **legal@gotchaa.com** including:

1. Your signature  
2. Identification of removed material and its prior location  
3. Statement under penalty of perjury of good-faith belief of mistake or misidentification  
4. Name, address, phone, email  
5. Consent to jurisdiction of Federal District Court for your address (or where GOTCHAA is located if outside US)  
6. Consent to accept service of process from the complainant  

We may restore content in **10–14 business days** unless the complainant files court action.

## 5. Repeat Infringer Policy

Accounts with **three** valid DMCA strikes in 12 months may be permanently terminated.

## 6. Misrepresentation

Knowingly submitting false claims may result in liability under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

## 7. Non-US Users

We honor equivalent copyright notices under local law where applicable.
